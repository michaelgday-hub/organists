Dual-Target Engraving Architecture

Every score in the Public Organ Library is engraved from verified public-domain MusicXML sources using LilyPond 2.26.0 and compiled simultaneously into two purpose-built PDF formats:

  • Tablet Edition (6mm Symmetric Margins): Optimized for 12.9-inch iPad Pro displays and forScore. Maximizes staff size and note readability on the music rack with single-sided page turns.
  • Print Edition (12mm Inner Binding Gutter): Formatted for two-sided duplex printing and spiral or comb binding, ensuring inner margins never clip measures near the spine.

Access & Chapter Support

The Public Organ Library provides complimentary access to active members of the Chambersburg Chapter of the American Guild of Organists (AGO) as well as organ students and faculty holding verified .edu academic addresses.

Sustaining Patrons and visiting church musicians are welcome to browse our open-access catalog and submit clean, 3-staff MusicXML (.mxl) transcriptions for archival engraving.
